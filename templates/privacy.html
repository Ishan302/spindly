Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

01Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not ask for your bank credentials or payment card details.

02How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to provide and improve Spendly: to log you in, to show your expenses and spending summaries, and to keep the service running reliably. We do not sell your personal information or use your expense data for advertising.

03Data Storage

Your data is stored in our application database and is accessible only to you through your account. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it, such as hashing passwords, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You can request deletion of your account and data at any time.

04Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when you visit our pages. We do not share your expense data with any third party. If we add other services in the future, we will update this policy to describe them.

05Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request access to or deletion of your data, contact us at support@spendly.example.
